Our story

Built on the factory floor, not in a boardroom

VeniXtus QMS exists because paper checklists and scattered spreadsheets were not good enough for the plants we have worked in.

VeniXtus QMS was founded by a quality assurance and SHEQ professional with more than eight years of hands-on experience in food manufacturing, dairy and aviation catering, the kind of environments where a missed critical control point check or a lost audit trail has real consequences.

After years of watching good QA teams get buried under paper checklists, disconnected spreadsheets and findings that quietly went nowhere, we set out to build the platform we wished we had: one place where an audit finding becomes a CAPA, a CAPA links to a risk, and every one of them surfaces at the next management review.

VENIXTUS (Pty) Ltd is based in Randburg, Johannesburg, South Africa, and the platform is designed around South African requirements such as the OHS Act and SAHPRA guidance, alongside international standards.

How we got here

From checklist to platform

The problem

Years of QA and food safety work exposed the same gap everywhere: audits, CAPA and risk lived in separate tools, or in no tool at all.

The first build

A single-purpose audit checklist app, built to solve one real problem at a time so no finding is lost and no CAPA forgotten.

Connecting the dots

Risk registers, document control, supplier quality, laboratory, occupational health and safety and more joined the platform, each feeding the others.

Controls that are enforced

Approvals by a second person, locked records, permanent history and controlled workflows for regulatory reporting, validation and design, applied on the server rather than left to good habits.

VeniXtus QMS

In 2026 the product became VeniXtus QMS, operated by VENIXTUS (Pty) Ltd.
