Common questions

Pricing FAQ

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No. The 30-day trial gives full access to every module with no card required.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Your data stays with you regardless of plan.

Does it work without internet?

You can capture some records offline. They sync when you are back online. Approvals and other server-checked actions need a connection.
