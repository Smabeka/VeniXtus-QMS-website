About the platform

One system instead of five spreadsheets

VeniXtus QMS is an integrated digital management platform that helps businesses manage quality, health and safety, food safety, environmental risk, audits, documentation and regulatory compliance in one centralised system.

An audit finding becomes a CAPA. A CAPA links to a risk. Both surface at the next management review. Approvals are done by the right person, signed, and kept in a permanent history that nobody can quietly edit afterwards.

It is built for manufacturing, food production, construction, engineering, logistics, healthcare and professional services, and it runs in a browser on your phone or computer with nothing to install.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. VeniXtus QMS runs in your browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen for an app-like experience, with no app store needed.

Does it work without internet?

You can open the app and capture some records while offline. Records sync to the cloud when you are connected again, and anything not yet synced stays on that device until it does.

Which standards does it support?

The Standards & Compliance Framework module lets you map requirements to clauses of frameworks such as ISO 9001, ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 13485, ISO/IEC 17025, BRCGS and HACCP, together with South African occupational health and food safety requirements.

Can several people work together?

Yes. Each person has a role (Admin, Quality Manager, Approver, Department Manager, Auditor or Employee), and important approvals must be given by someone other than the person who prepared the record.

Is my data secure and separate from other companies?

Each organisation's data is kept separate in a cloud database with access rules enforced on the server. See our Privacy Policy for how personal information is handled.

Yes. Every plan starts with a 30-day free trial and no credit card. See Pricing.
